As a new build, this project is well positioned to achieve Passive House levels of airtightness. The dedicated air
barrier layers described above, carefully detailed and installed with taped seams and sealed penetrations, should be the
primary strategy and are expected to provide excellent, durable airtightness. Even so, complex junctions, service
penetrations, and trade sequencing can leave small leaks that are difficult to locate once finishes are installed. We
recommend a mid-construction blower-door test while the air barrier is still accessible, and having a backup plan in
place should results fall short of the target.

[AeroBarrier](https://aeroseal.com/industries/residential-new-construction/) provides whole-building air sealing using
an aerosolized polymer that "finds" leaks and seals them. We recommend consulting early in the build with an AeroBarrier
installer so that they can be included in the project schedule should their services be required.
