title: "Airtightness Product Compatibility"
dek: "Choosing air barrier products that work together."
---

An air barrier is only as good as its connections. Membranes, tapes, sealants, adhesives, and primers must be
compatible with each other and with the surfaces they are applied to. Incompatible products can fail to bond, or lose
adhesion over time, leaving leaks that are hidden behind the finishes.

We recommend using airtightness products from a single manufacturer and product family wherever possible. The
assemblies in this report use the [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) membrane at the walls and
roofs, so the tapes, sealants, and primers used with it (for example at seams, penetrations, windows, and connections
to the concrete foundation walls) should come from the same pro clima product family. Some manufacturers only
warrant their products when they are used together as a system.

Where products from different manufacturers must meet, such as where the wall and roof air barrier connects to the
sub-slab vapor barrier (Stego) or the concrete foundation walls, confirm compatibility with both manufacturers, follow
their requirements for primers and surface preparation, and test adhesion on a small mock-up before full installation.
