- Effective assembly R-value: **R-31.3** (U-0.032 Btu/hr·ft²·°F), not including the thermal resistance of the
  surrounding soil.
- Note: All concrete sizing as per Structural Engineer. Concrete is shown for illustration purposes only.
- Target 6" sub-slab graphite-enhanced EPS (GPS, R-5/in), such as Neopor, or sim.
- Install foam insulation in multiple layers with all seams staggered (offset board edges min 12"), fill all voids
  with low-expansion spray foam.
- Sub-slab air and vapor barrier to be installed directly below concrete (above insulation). Use [15 Mil
  StegoHome](https://475.supply/products/stegohome-15-mil-vapor-barrier) or similar
- Tape / Seal all penetrations and seams of sub-slab air and vapor barrier with
  [StegoTape](https://475.supply/products/stego-tape) / [StegoMastic](https://475.supply/products/stego-mastic) or
  similar. Coordinate and seal any radon mitigation piping penetrations.
- As-drawn assembly (R-25.3) for comparison: [S1 as drawn (PDF)](/assets/envelope/assemblies/S1.pdf)
