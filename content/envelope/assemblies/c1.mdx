- Effective assembly R-value: **R-28.5** (U-0.035 Btu/hr·ft²·°F), not including the thermal resistance of the
  surrounding soil.
- Note: All concrete sizing as per Structural Engineer. Concrete is shown for illustration purposes only.
- 3" graphite-enhanced EPS (GPS, R-5/in), such as Neopor, or sim. continuous exterior insulation, rated for
  below-grade use. Waterproofing / damp-proofing as per Architect.
- Concrete foundation wall serves as the primary air-barrier. Seal all form-tie holes, cold joints, and penetrations to
  ensure air-tight.
- 2x4 interior stud wall insulated with 3-1/2" Rockwool Comfortbatt. Use for all plumbing and electrical to avoid
  penetrating the concrete air-barrier.
