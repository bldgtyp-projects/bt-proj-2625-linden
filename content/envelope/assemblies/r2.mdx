- Effective assembly R-value: **R-75.7** (U-0.013 Btu/hr·ft²·°F).
- Note: All structural member sizing and spacing as per Structural Engineer. 12" engineered-lumber joists are shown for
  illustration purposes only.
- Install 4" continuous polyisocyanurate (polyiso) insulation above the roof sheathing. Thermal bridging from metal
  fasteners is included in the R-value above; use adhered insulation where possible to reduce fastener penetrations.
- Fill joist bays with dense-pack cellulose.
- Note: when constructing un-vented insulated roof assemblies follow all code requirements as per IRC 2024 Section
  R806.5. Climate Zone 5 requires min. R-20 rigid insulation above the roof sheathing; 4" polyiso provides approx.
  R-23.
- Primary air-barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor-retarder
  installed along underside of all roof joists. Tape all seams to ensure air and moisture-vapor tight.
- 2x4 service cavity below the air/vapor retarder membrane, insulated with 3-1/2" Rockwool Comfortbatt. Use for all
  plumbing, electrical, and / or lighting fixtures.
