- Effective assembly R-value: **R-64.9** (U-0.015 Btu/hr·ft²·°F).
- Note: All structural member sizing and spacing as per Structural Engineer. 12" engineered-lumber joists are shown for
  illustration purposes only.
- Install 4" continuous polyisocyanurate (polyiso) insulation above the roof sheathing, with a rated R-value of at
  least R-24 in total (R-6.0 per inch). Thermal bridging from metal fasteners is included in the R-value above; use
  adhered insulation where possible to reduce fastener penetrations.
- Note: the 4" shown is the minimum thickness. Any sloped (tapered) insulation added above it for drainage counts
  toward the roof's total, since Phius allows the average insulation thickness to be used. This will raise both the
  roof's R-value and the share of insulation above the sheathing.
- Fill joist bays with dense-pack cellulose.
- Note: for an unvented roof in Climate Zone 5, Phius requires the insulation above the roof sheathing to make up more
  than 35% of the roof's total insulation R-value, which keeps the sheathing warm enough to stay dry. This assembly
  just meets that (about R-24 of about R-68), so do not substitute a lower-rated polyiso or add insulation below the
  roof sheathing without rechecking it. The code minimum is R-20 above the sheathing (IRC 2024 Section R806.5).
- Primary air-barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor-retarder
  installed along underside of all roof joists. Tape all seams to ensure air and moisture-vapor tight.
- 2x4 service cavity below the air/vapor retarder membrane, left uninsulated. Use for all plumbing, electrical, and /
  or lighting fixtures. Do not insulate this cavity: added insulation below the sheathing would take the roof out of
  compliance with the Phius rule above.
