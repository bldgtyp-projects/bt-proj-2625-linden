- Effective assembly R-value: **R-55.2** (U-0.018 Btu/hr·ft²·°F).
- Note: All structural member sizing and spacing as per Structural Engineer. 14" engineered-lumber rafters are shown for
  illustration purposes only.
- Un-vented roof assembly. Install 4" closed-cell spray foam (ccSPF) directly to the underside of the roof sheathing, and
  fill the remainder of the rafter bay with dense-pack cellulose.
- Note: when constructing un-vented insulated rafter assemblies follow all code requirements as per IRC 2024 Section
  R806.5. Climate Zone 5 requires min. R-20 air-impermeable insulation in direct contact with the underside of the
  sheathing; 4" ccSPF provides approx. R-24.
- Primary air-barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor-retarder
  installed along underside of all rafters. Tape all seams to ensure air and moisture-vapor tight.
- 2x4 service cavity below the air/vapor retarder membrane, insulated with 3-1/2" Rockwool Comfortbatt. Use for all
  plumbing, electrical, and / or lighting fixtures.
