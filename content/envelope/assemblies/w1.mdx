- Effective assembly R-value: **R-36.2** (U-0.028 Btu/hr·ft²·°F).
- 2" [Rockwool Comfortboard 80](https://www.rockwool.com/north-america/products-and-applications/products/comfortboard-80/)
  continuous exterior mineral-wool insulation over 1/2" OSB sheathing. Use a ventilated rainscreen cladding outboard of
  the Comfortboard.
- 2x6 stud wall at 16" o.c., full-depth dense-pack cellulose insulation.
- Primary air-barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor-retarder
  installed on the interior face of the 2x6 studs. Tape all seams and penetrations to ensure air and moisture-vapor
  tight.
- 2-1/2" service cavity installed inside of the air-barrier, insulated with mineral wool batts.
- Use 'Service Cavity' on interior side for all plumbing and electrical. Reduce penetrations through the air-barrier to
  improve building airtightness.
