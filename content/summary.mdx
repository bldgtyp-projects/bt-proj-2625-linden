This report evaluates five design variants for the home, from a Boulder energy code baseline up to a Passive House
Institute (PHI) Low Energy Building.

**We recommend the PHI Low Energy Building variant.** It is the only variant that meets the PHI heating demand limit.
Compared with the code-minimum baseline, it reduces modeled site energy by about 60% (from roughly 49,900 to 19,100
kWh per year) and the peak heating load by about 65% (from roughly 119 to 41 kBtu/h).

Key upgrades beyond the current drawings:

- Best-in-class triple-pane windows (installed U-value of approximately 0.14 Btu/hr·ft²·°F).
- Approximately R-30 basement walls and floor slab.
- 4" polyiso above the flat roof deck (approximately R-76).
- A high-efficiency energy recovery ventilator (ERV).
- An airtightness target of 0.6 ACH50.

performance and shading, and the mechanical and ventilation systems.
