The results in this report are based on the following sources:

- **Energy model:** PHPP version 10.6, with five design variants as described in the [Model Variants](/energy_model/#model-variants)
  section.
- **Drawings:** building geometry is based on the drawings and models received on
  <Var k="source_files.cad_files_received_date" />.
- **Climate data:** <Var k="narrative.climate.weather_station_name" />.
- **Assembly R-values:** effective whole-assembly values from 2D heat-flow (Flixo) models, including thermal bridging
  through framing and fasteners. The model output for each assembly is linked in the
  [Recommended Assemblies](/building_envelope/#recommended-assemblies) section.
- **Carbon emissions:** <Var k="narrative.co2.subregion_name" /> emission rates.
