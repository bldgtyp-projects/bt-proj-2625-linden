As shown in the results below, in order to assess the building performance we have tested the building in five distinct
configurations (variants):

1. **Code-Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the **<VarLink
hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink>** code minimums for assembly
U-values, window U-values, airtightness and equipment efficiencies. This variant assumes a basic balanced ventilation
system with heat recovery (HRV), as required by the Boulder code, and that the building uses all-electric appliances
and equipment for heating, cooling, hot-water and cooking.

2. **Drawn Insulation:** This variant uses the assembly R-values shown in the current design drawings (approximately R-36
walls, R-52 sloped roof, R-65 flat roof, R-25 basement floor, and R-21 basement walls) along with improved thermal-bridge detailing. Windows,
airtightness (3.0 ACH50), and the basic HRV remain at the code-minimum levels.

3. **Airtight + ERV:** Building on the Drawn Insulation variant, this version improves the building airtightness to 1.0
ACH50 and upgrades the ventilation to a high-performance energy recovery ventilator (ERV), at roughly 80% heat
recovery.

4. **PH Windows:** Building on the Airtight + ERV variant, this version replaces the code-minimum windows with
high-performance, standard Passive House-grade triple-pane windows (installed U-value of approximately 0.17 Btu/hr-ft2-F).

5. **PHI Low Energy Building [RECOMMENDED]:** A version which meets the Passive House Institute (PHI) Low Energy Building
certification criteria. Relative to the PH Windows variant, this version includes best in class windows (U-value of
approximately 0.14 Btu/hr-ft2-F, e.g. Smartwin), increases the basement wall and basement floor insulation to
approximately R-30 (3" exterior and 6" sub-slab graphite-enhanced EPS), increases the flat roof to approximately R-76
(increasing the polyiso above the roof deck from 2" to 4"), and tightens the airtightness target to 0.6 ACH50. This is the only variant which
meets the PHI Low Energy Building heating demand limit of 9.5 kBtu/ft2 per year (30 kWh/m2), with a modeled heating demand
of about 9.2 kBtu/ft2 per year (29 kWh/m2). Because this margin is small (about 3%), design changes to the windows, shading, insulation, or
airtightness should be checked against the energy model.
