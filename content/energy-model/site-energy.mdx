"Site Energy" represents the energy purchased by the building and delivered to the site by the utility. This is the most
typical energy use figure assessed when considering a site "Net-Zero" building energy balance, or when considering the
annual cost of energy for the building. This site-energy total is made up of 6 main groups: heating, cooling, hot-water,
lighting, electric equipment (appliances, plug loads, etc.), and pumps / fans.

In order to assess the performance of the home across a range of options, we have simulated 6 distinct variants with
different energy efficiency measures (see the [Model Variants](#model-variants) section for all the details on the
specific variant inputs). Compared with the Code Min variant, the recommended Phius ZERO variant reduces modeled site
energy use by about 43%, from about 36,900 to 21,200 kWh/yr. The Phius CORE and Phius ZERO variants use the same
amount of energy as the + Shading variant; they differ only in their solar panels, which produce about 3,700 and
23,300 kWh/yr respectively.

The solar production in the Phius ZERO variant is sized to meet the Phius source energy calculation, which uses
standard assumptions for occupancy and typical household energy use. Actual energy use will depend on the household,
and loads that are not part of the certification calculation, such as electric vehicle charging, are not included
here.
