#### Swing tank

If a hot water recirculation system is used with a heat pump water heater, a swing tank design should be used. The
recirculation return goes to a small electric-resistance swing tank rather than back to the heat pump tank, so the
heat pump is not forced to reheat lukewarm return water. This keeps the heat pump running at its best efficiency while
also adding hot water capacity to the system.

#### Drain-water heat recovery

To reduce hot water energy use and extend the capacity of the hot water tank, we recommend installing an in-line
drain-water heat recovery unit on the shower drains. This simple element has no moving parts and recovers heat from the
shower drain water that would otherwise be lost to the sewer, typically saving 15-25% of the energy in that water.
Given the modest first cost and quick payback, we strongly recommend installing a product of this sort. Some product
options to review include:

- [RenewABILITY Power-Pipe](https://renewability.com/)
- [EcoInnovation ThermoDrain](https://ecoinnovation.ca/)
- [ecodrain](https://ecodrain.com/en/)
