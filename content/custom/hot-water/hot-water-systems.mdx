dek: "Why hot water becomes a major energy use in a Passive House."
---

Once the heating demand is reduced through Passive House strategies, domestic hot water (DHW) becomes one of the
largest remaining energy uses. In the recommended PHI Low Energy Building variant, hot water accounts for about 3,650
kWh per year, or roughly 19% of the home's total site energy, second only to space heating. An efficient hot water
system can make a significant difference to the building's overall energy use and carbon emissions.

The energy model assumes an electric heat pump water heater for all variants. The following sections describe
suitable water heaters, recirculation and drain-water heat recovery, and the PHI requirements for the hot water system.
