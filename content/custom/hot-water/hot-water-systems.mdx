title: "Hot Water Systems"
dek: "Why hot water matters in a low-energy home."
---

Once the heating demand is reduced through passive building strategies, domestic hot water (DHW) becomes a
significant share of the energy the house needs. In the recommended Phius ZERO variant, heating the water takes about
3,600 kWh of heat per year. Because the heat pump water heater delivers about two and a half units of heat for each
unit of electricity it uses, this costs only about 1,400 kWh of electricity per year, or roughly 7% of the home's
total site energy. A less efficient water heater, or long, poorly insulated piping, would increase this quickly, so an
efficient system still makes a significant difference to the building's overall energy use and carbon emissions.

The energy model assumes an electric heat pump water heater for all variants. The following sections describe
suitable water heaters, recirculation and drain-water heat recovery, and the Phius requirements for the hot water
system.
