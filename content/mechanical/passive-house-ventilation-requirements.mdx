The following ventilation design and commissioning guidelines are drawn from the Phius certification requirements and
the ENERGY STAR, DOE Zero Energy Ready Home, and EPA Indoor airPLUS programs that Phius requires alongside it. Most are
required for Phius certification; the rest we recommend for this project as good practice.

#### Design requirements

- Outdoor-air supply is at least 15 cfm per person (about 90 cfm for the six occupants used in the Phius
  calculation), and every bedroom receives its own supply of outdoor air.
- Continuous exhaust is at least 20 cfm per bathroom and 25 cfm for the kitchen, or intermittent exhaust is at least
  50 cfm per bathroom and 100 cfm for the kitchen range hood. Half-baths and powder rooms count as full bathrooms.
- Total supply and exhaust airflows are balanced within 10% of each other, using the higher airflow as the basis of
  the percentage difference, or the ventilation does not pressurize or depressurize the house by more than 5 Pa.
- Ventilation air comes directly from outdoors, not from garages, crawlspaces, or attics.
- All ventilation air inlets are located at least 10 ft stretched-string distance from known contamination sources,
  and at least 4 ft above grade or the roof surface.
- All ventilation air inlets are located at least 5 ft from ventilation exhaust outlets; 10 ft is recommended.
- Outdoor air passes through a filter of at least MERV 8 before it is distributed; we recommend MERV 13 or better,
  particularly for wildfire smoke. The filter is located for easy regular service, it is changed at the end of
  construction, and the building is ventilated before occupancy.
- Ventilation ducts between the ERVs and the outside are insulated to at least R-8, with an air-sealed Class I vapor
  retarder over any air-permeable insulation, such as fiberglass duct wrap.
- The mechanical drawings show the duct layout and sizes, every supply and exhaust register with its airflow, filters,
  exterior penetrations, duct insulation, and the duct lengths from the exterior wall or roof to each ERV.

#### Commissioning requirements

- The system is tested and balanced room by room before occupancy by the Phius Rater or Verifier, or by a qualified
  balancing contractor under their review.
- Total supply and exhaust each reach at least 100% of the design airflow at normal operation, and remain within 10% of
  each other.
- Each tested room reaches at least 100% of its design airflow, and does not exceed it by more than 20% or 5 cfm,
  whichever is larger.
- With the bedroom doors closed and the ventilation at its normal setting, each bedroom is within 1 Pa of the main
  living space, so that the supply air is not blocked by a closed door.
- The ERV fan power is measured at normal operation after commissioning, with clean filters.
