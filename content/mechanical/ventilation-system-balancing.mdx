For this building, we recommend a high-performance balanced ERV system with better than 75% heat recovery, which will
provide excellent indoor air quality while minimizing energy consumption and occupant comfort issues. Given the size of
the house, we assume two units will be required to meet the airflow and distribution requirements. The units should be
outfitted with high-quality air filtration, MERV 13 or better, which will be critical to ensuring clean and healthy
indoor air. Additional filtration may also be desired to manage wildfire smoke.

The values used in this analysis are assumed only. We recommend contacting both Zehnder and Brink for design
assistance. The selected manufacturer or their representative should be retained to fully design, specify, and balance
the system after installation. This is critical for proper operation and required for all high-performance buildings.
#### System balancing

Prior to occupancy, the fresh-air system should be tested and balanced to ensure good air mixing, adequate supply to
all living spaces, and compliance with the certification requirements. Some ERV vendors provide this balancing as part of their services; if not, a third-party
commissioning agent may be needed.
